Take out only the pages you need

This tool creates a new PDF that contains just the pages you choose. Typical reasons are sending only the signed page of a contract, sharing one chapter of a long report, attaching the single relevant page of a bank statement, or dropping blank pages that a scanner added.

How page selection works

  1. Open your PDF. Every page appears as a thumbnail, and all pages start out selected.
  2. Click a thumbnail to deselect a page you do not want. Click it again to select it again.
  3. Press split. The new split.pdf contains the selected pages in their original order.

Removing pages is the same as selecting the ones you keep: to delete two blank pages from a 30-page scan, just click those two thumbnails and split.

Splitting into several files

Each run produces one PDF. To divide a document into parts — for example chapters 1–3 and 4–6 — run the tool once per part, choosing a different set of pages each time.

What the new PDF keeps

  • Pages are copied exactly: text remains searchable and images keep their quality.
  • Page size and orientation stay the same as in the original.
  • Your original file is not changed.

Need to fix the direction of a page as well? Use Rotate PDF. To turn selected pages into images, use PDF to JPG.

Questions about splitting PDFs

Can I change the page order?

No. Pages keep their original order. To build a new order, split the parts you need and join them with Merge PDF in the order you want.

Does the PDF leave my computer?

No. Pages are read and copied inside your browser.
