GS1 2027 barcode transition: what QR users need to know

GS1 released a retail-system readiness playbook in March 2026 for the transition toward compatible 2D codes at checkout by the end of 2027.

Source: March 2026 · Convertios publication: 11 Nov 2026

The 2027 goal

GS1’s March 2026 playbook describes how retail point-of-sale systems can prepare to read GS1-compliant 2D barcodes alongside existing linear barcodes. It is an industry goal, not proof every store is ready today.

More than a QR scanner

A checkout may need both compatible scanner hardware and POS or backend software that understands GS1 identifiers.

A regular QR code is different

A QR image that simply contains a website address is great for menus or marketing; it does not automatically meet GS1 retail product-identification requirements.

Practical preparation

Test real packaging codes with retail partners. For promotional QR codes, test the destination, print contrast and quiet zone before distribution.
