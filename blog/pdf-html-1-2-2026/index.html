PDF Association updates its PDF-to-HTML algorithm to version 1.2

A September 2026 technical update brings better structure mapping, mathematics handling, and clearer accessibility guidance to PDF-to-HTML conversion.

Source: 27 September 2026 · Convertios publication: 11 Nov 2026

What changed

On September 27, 2026, the PDF Association published version 1.2 of its Deriving PDF to HTML algorithm. The document aligns more closely with Well-Tagged PDF, including semantic elements, math content and predictable HTML output.

More than copying how a page looks

Moving from PDF to HTML means translating document structure, not simply taking screenshots of pages. Headings, formulas, references and reading order require special handling.

What accessibility means here

The revised specification clarifies that accessible HTML is not automatically equivalent to accessible source PDF. Implementers still need to test actual reading behavior.

What users can do today

When converting documents between formats, inspect structure, links, tables and mathematics in the output. Convertios offers other PDF tasks, but does not claim to implement this PDF-to-HTML algorithm.
