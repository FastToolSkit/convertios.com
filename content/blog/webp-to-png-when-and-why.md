## Why so many images are WebP

Websites use WebP because it usually produces smaller files than JPEG or PNG at similar visual quality, which makes pages load faster. All current major browsers display it. The friction appears outside the browser: some older image editors, office programs, printing services and upload forms still expect JPG or PNG.

## When converting to PNG makes sense

- **The image has transparency**, such as a logo or a cut-out product photo. PNG keeps transparent areas; JPG does not.
- **You will edit it further.** PNG is lossless, so saving it repeatedly while editing does not add new compression damage.
- **It is a graphic, screenshot or diagram** with sharp edges and text, which PNG handles well.
- **A tool or form only accepts PNG.**

## When JPG is the better target

For ordinary photographs without transparency, JPG is usually better than PNG. A photo saved as PNG can be several times larger than the WebP it came from, because PNG stores every pixel without lossy compression. Use the [Image Converter](tool:image-converter) to produce JPG instead, or see [PNG to JPG and transparency](blog:png-to-jpg-transparency) for what happens to transparent areas.

## What conversion does and does not change

- **Quality cannot go up.** If the WebP was saved with lossy compression, its artefacts are already part of the image. Converting to PNG preserves the image exactly as it is now, artefacts included.
- **File size usually goes up** with PNG, sometimes a lot. That is the price of lossless storage.
- **Dimensions stay the same.** To make it smaller in pixels, resize separately.
- **Animated WebP** files are a special case: converting to PNG produces a still image.

## How to convert with Convertios

1. Open [WebP to PNG](tool:webp-to-png). It runs in your browser, so images are not uploaded.
2. Choose one or more .webp files.
3. Download the PNG versions and check that transparent areas look right.

If the resulting PNG is too large for an upload limit, convert to JPG instead, or reduce the size with the [Image Compressor](tool:compress-image), which can aim for a target size in KB.

## Avoiding WebP downloads in the first place

If you regularly need JPG or PNG, check whether the website offers a download button; original files are often available in their original format. For your own images, export from your editor or phone directly in the format you need rather than converting a web copy, which may have been resized and recompressed for the page.

## Respect image rights

Being able to save an image from a website does not mean you may reuse it. Product photos, artwork and photographs are usually protected by copyright. Convert images you own, have permission to use, or that are clearly licensed for reuse.

- **تبقى الأبعاد كما هي.** ولتصغيرها بالبكسل غيّر حجمها بخطوة منفصلة.
