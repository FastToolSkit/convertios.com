Extract MP3 audio from a video

Use this tool when you need the speech, music or soundtrack from a video as a separate audio file. Convertios removes the video stream and encodes the available audio track as a 192 kbps MP3 that works with common phones, computers, editors and music players.

Compatible input formats

You can upload MP4, MOV, WEBM, MKV, AVI, M4V, MPEG, MPG, 3GP, OGV, TS, FLV and WMV video files. The source must contain an audio track. A silent video cannot produce useful MP3 audio.

How to convert video to MP3

  1. Select or drag a video up to 50 MB into the upload area.
  2. Press Convert to MP3 and keep the page open.
  3. Wait while FFmpeg extracts and encodes the audio.
  4. The resulting MP3 downloads automatically.

Privacy and file deletion

Video extraction runs on the self-hosted Convertios backend. Files exist only inside a temporary processing directory and are automatically removed after the request finishes. We do not build a media library, retain uploads or use your content for training.

Frequently asked questions

What MP3 quality will I receive?

The output uses a 192 kbps MP3 bitrate, balancing clear audio with a practical file size.

Does this tool download videos from websites?

No. It converts a video file that you own and select from your device. It does not download media from third-party URLs.

Are uploaded files stored?

No. Temporary input and output files are deleted automatically after processing.

Why did a video fail?

The video may be damaged, lack an audio stream, use an unsupported codec or exceed the 50 MB limit.
