AI Image Enhancer FAQ

What does the AI Image Enhancer do?

It improves image quality, sharpness, and clarity using an AI enhancement workflow, then shows the enhanced image for preview and download.

Is it free to use?

Yes. The AI Image Enhancer is available as a free Convertios tool.

Are my images uploaded?

Yes. Your selected image is uploaded to Cloudinary using an unsigned upload preset, and the resulting public image URL is sent to the n8n AI enhancement workflow.

What image formats are supported?

Common web image formats such as JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported, depending on the Cloudinary upload preset and browser file selection.

Why does enhancement take time?

The tool first uploads your image and then waits for the AI workflow to process it. Larger images or temporary provider load can increase processing time.

What should I do if enhancement fails?

Try a smaller JPG, PNG, or WEBP file, check your connection, and retry. If the issue continues, the Cloudinary preset or n8n workflow may need configuration.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The tool works in modern mobile browsers, though upload and processing speed depends on your device, image size, and network connection.
