AI Image Enhancer FAQ

What does the AI Image Enhancer do?

It improves image quality, sharpness, and clarity using an AI enhancement workflow, then shows the enhanced image for preview and download.

Is it free to use?

Yes. The AI Image Enhancer is available as a free Convertios tool.

Are my images uploaded?

Yes. The image is uploaded to the Convertios server, which sends it to Cloudinary for AI restoration and sharpening (small images are also upscaled 4×) and returns the result to you. Use a browser-only tool for private images.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WEBP images up to 8 MB are supported.

Why does enhancement take time?

The tool first uploads your image and then waits for the AI enhancement to process it. Larger images or temporary provider load can increase processing time.

What should I do if enhancement fails?

Try a smaller JPG, PNG or WEBP image, check your connection and retry. If the enhancement service is temporarily unavailable, the page tells you so; try again a little later.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The tool works in modern mobile browsers, though upload and processing speed depends on your device, image size, and network connection.
