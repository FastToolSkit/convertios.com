The tool is designed to be simple and intuitive, allowing you to edit images quickly without any technical knowledge.

Resize for the destination, not just a number

For a website thumbnail, choose dimensions suited to the displayed size; for a profile picture, check the platform’s aspect ratio and cropping requirements. Enlarging a small original can make it look soft or pixelated.

Why resize and crop images?

Resizing and cropping images is essential for many online tasks. Different platforms require specific dimensions, and properly sized images help improve appearance, loading speed, and overall performance. Cropping allows you to focus on the most important part of an image while removing unnecessary elements.

Optimizing images also reduces file size, making them easier to upload and share across websites and applications.

Checks and limitations

Keep an untouched source image. Inspect faces, logos and text after resizing or cropping to make sure important details were not cut off.
