Why convert JPG to PNG?

JPG files are compressed and commonly used for photos, but they may lose some quality during compression. PNG files, on the other hand, use lossless compression and maintain better image detail.

Converting JPG to PNG is useful when you need higher quality images, transparency support, or better results for editing and design work.
