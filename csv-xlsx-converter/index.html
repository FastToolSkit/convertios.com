Convert CSV to XLSX or Excel to CSV

Create an XLSX workbook from CSV or export one selected Excel sheet to CSV. Choose delimiter and encoding, and review how text and formulas are handled.

Processed temporarily on the server; uploaded files are deleted after the job.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose CSV to XLSX or XLSX to CSV and select the file.
  2. Set delimiter and encoding; for Excel input, wait for the sheet list and choose one sheet.
  3. Convert and download the resulting workbook or CSV file.

Practical example

Turn a semicolon-separated product list into an XLSX sheet, or export a single workbook tab for another system.

Understanding the result

CSV cells become text in XLSX. Existing workbook formulas are not calculated; CSV export uses cached values when available.

Options and workflow

Choose the direction, delimiter and text encoding. For XLSX input, upload the workbook to load its sheet names, select one sheet, then convert. CSV cells are stored as text in XLSX.

Limits and privacy

CSV input is limited to 4 MiB, XLSX to 6 MiB, 10,000 rows and 100 columns. XLSX ZIP expansion is checked before parsing. Formula cells in XLSX export their cached result, when present; CSV output prefixes formula-like text for safer opening. Files are deleted after server processing.

Common questions

Are formulas executed?

No. Imported CSV cells stay text. Existing workbook formulas are not evaluated; only cached values are exported to CSV.

Will every Excel sheet be exported?

No. Select one sheet after upload; only that sheet is written to CSV.
