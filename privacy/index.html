Privacy Policy

At Convertios, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Convertios does not require user registration or ask for personal information such as your name or email address to use its tools. Many tools process files locally in your browser without uploading them. Tools that require advanced conversion may temporarily upload the selected file to our processing backend solely to complete the requested operation.

How We Use Information

We may use anonymous data such as browser type or usage behavior to improve performance and user experience.

Cookies

Convertios may use cookies to enhance functionality and analyze website traffic. Cookies are small data files stored on your device.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Google AdSense to display advertisements. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads.

Data Security

For browser-based tools, files remain on your device. For backend-based tools, uploaded files and generated results are stored only in a temporary processing directory and are automatically deleted when the request finishes, whether the conversion succeeds or fails. Convertios does not use uploaded files for advertising, profiling, resale, or AI training. If a tool uses a third-party processing service, that processing is disclosed on the relevant tool page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please visit our contact page.
